# Jump HQ: SmartSuli AI Hackathon pitch

**One line:** An AI team that finds your clients and runs your social media. It's for Sulaimani's online businesses and freelancers, works 24/7 in Kurdish, Arabic and English, and you approve everything from your phone.

---

## The problem (one, specific, Sulaimani)

Many people in Sulaimani already earn online: AI video ad makers, designers, marketers, developers and data analysts, plus Instagram, TikTok and Facebook shops. They are good at their work. **Growing** is what eats their time.

- **Freelancers and agencies:** every new client means hours of searching, checking the business, making a sample and writing a good message in Sorani. Upwork, Fiverr and Stripe payouts don't work from Iraq, so local and regional clients are the market. Most give up after a few tries.
- **Online shops and brands:** they sell through their pages, and sales follow posting. Posting every day on four apps is a full-time job most of them can't pay someone for.

**Problem in one sentence:** Sulaimani's online sellers lose most of their time to finding clients and keeping their pages alive, instead of doing the work they're good at.

## Proof the money is real (the mentor's question)

We don't sell to shops that have never sold online. Our customers **already earn online**, so one client won (or one good month of posts) pays for a year of Jump HQ.
**We are customer number one:** from Sulaimani, our founder makes AI video ads for a US brand and is paid every month. Making the ads was never the hard part. Finding the next client and keeping posts going was.

## The solution: two modes, one team

### Mode 1: Find clients
You tell a setup chat what you sell (2 minutes). Every agent adapts: the search, the scoring, the free sample, the message and the prices.

| Step | Agent | Exactly what it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scout | Looks for **buying signals** in Kurdish, Arabic and English: public posts asking for your kind of service, brands already paying for ads (Meta Ad Library, sponsored posts) whose ads are weak, online shops whose pages went quiet, hiring posts. It prefers businesses that already sell online. |
| 2 | Investigator | Proves they're real: followers, recent posts, public contact. Every fact is marked *verified* or *assumed*. Nothing is invented. |
| 3 | Analyst | Scores 0–100 for need, budget and how easy they are to reach. Only the best go on. |
| 4 | Builder | Makes a **free sample of your work** for them: an ad concept (script and storyboard), a store page, a sample data report, a clickable app prototype or a proposal. |
| 5 | Writer | A short, honest WhatsApp message in natural Sorani with one link: the sample. |
| 6 | Closer | When they reply, drafts the answer until they say yes. |
| ⚖ | **Judge** | Independent AI reviewer: checks every sample, message and post against the verified facts before it can go out. |

### Mode 2: Grow (built at the hackathon)
For online shops, brands and the freelancers themselves.
- The **Content creator** agent plans the next posts from what you sell and what works in your niche right now (it searches first).
- It writes the hook, the points and the call to action in Kurdish, Arabic or English. Jump HQ draws each one with its own professional templates: **1080×1350 picture posts** and **10-second vertical videos** (1080×1920, with motion, for Reels, TikTok and Stories). Text always fits, and Kurdish and Arabic posts are fully right-to-left.
- The **Judge** checks every caption and design: no invented prices, results or reviews, nothing that breaks platform rules.
- Approved posts go out **on schedule**, straight to your **Facebook Page** and **Telegram channel**. Instagram and TikTok: download and post with one tap. In Sleep mode, posts the Judge passes are scheduled by themselves.

**The owner does one thing: approve or reject, on the phone.**

## What is new (built during the 2 hackathon days)

1. **Grow mode:** the Content creator agent, picture posts and short videos, the Judge for posts, a content calendar and posting to Facebook Pages and Telegram channels.
2. **Setup for any business:** a setup chat tailors every agent to what you sell (ads, data, marketing, apps, websites).
3. **Sleep mode with an AI Judge.** Press Sleep (or schedule 23:00–08:00) and the team keeps working alone. Rules in code catch a wrong number, a missing link or spam. Then an AI review checks for invented facts, pressure or a wrong tone. Safe items go out, and anything risky waits for the morning.
   - *Real catches from testing:* it rewrote a message that praised "online booking in three steps" that nothing we know supports. It held a message that used a phone number that wasn't the business's own.
4. **Phone app** (Android). Pair it by scanning a QR code. It works on mobile data through a private link: Awake/Sleep, approve, preview, and send in WhatsApp with one tap.
5. **Whole product in English, Kurdish Sorani and Arabic.**
6. **Jump HQ Cloud:** a public sign-up site, a free week and monthly plans. Each customer gets a private Jump HQ, and the owner view shows revenue, AI cost and profit.
7. **Token saver:** the cheap model for most steps, merged checking steps and step limits per job.

*Honest note for the judges:* the search → sample pipeline was started on Oct 6. Everything in this list was built Oct 8–9 at the hackathon.

## Where the AI is (criterion 3)

AI does every step: finding buying signals, checking facts, scoring, planning, making the sample, planning and writing posts, writing in Sorani, answering replies and judging safety. The human only decides. Two independent AI layers (the Analyst before building, the Judge before anything goes out) keep it honest.

## Business model: Jump HQ Cloud (monthly subscription)

Sign up at the website, and your own private Jump HQ is ready in seconds. No install, no AI account. The agents run on **our** key, so every customer is recurring revenue, and plan limits cap what each one can cost us.

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| Free trial | $0 for 7 days | trying both modes |
| **Pro** | **$20 / month** (≈26,000 IQD) | one freelancer or one online shop: both modes, 25 team jobs a day |
| **Agency** | **$60 / month** (≈78,000 IQD) | agencies and teams: several brands, 80 team jobs a day |

Payment: FIB, card or bank transfer (works from Iraq).

**Our cost** (measured): about $0.11 per free sample, $0.05 per message, $0.03 per Judge check and about $0.16 for a batch of posts. A Pro user costs us about **$4–6 a month**, so $20 leaves **~70% margin**. Agency is ~3× the work for 3× the price.

**Why they pay:** one client won pays for more than a year of Pro.

### Round 2 (top 6, 5 minutes): what to add
The criteria change: **Public value, Innovation, Scale/adoption, Business model, Trust.**
- **Public value:** more Suli businesses online means more local income, and local freelancers stop depending on blocked foreign platforms.
- **Innovation:** not a chatbot. It's a full pipeline (find → verify → build → write → judge → send) that runs alone overnight, in Sorani.
- **Scale:** the market config is per country (`krd` now), so the next steps are Erbil and Duhok, then Iraq and the Gulf in Arabic. Any service business can use it, not only websites. An agency or a chamber of commerce could give it to its members.
- **Business model:** the subscription above, paid by FIB. What we need: Meta/TikTok app review for auto-posting, a small AI budget for the first users, and partners in seller groups.
- **Trust:** public business info only, never private people. Every fact is marked verified or assumed. A human or the Judge approves before anything is sent. There's a daily cap and an opt-out, and each customer's data is private.

## Likely judge questions

- **How exactly does it find clients?** Public buying signals: posts asking for the service, brands running ads, quiet shop pages, hiring posts, searched in three languages. Then every lead is checked and scored, and each fact is marked verified or assumed.
- **Is it spam?** No. Nothing is sent without the owner or the Judge. There's a daily cap, a clear opt-out, and it only uses the business's own public contact.
- **What if the AI invents facts?** The Analyst and the Judge both check against the verified facts. Samples say "concept, not the official site". Posts are held if they show invented prices or results.
- **Can it really post to Instagram and TikTok?** Facebook Pages and Telegram channels post automatically through their official APIs. Instagram and TikTok only allow automatic posting for approved business apps, so for now it's download and post in one tap. Automatic posting there is next, after Meta and TikTok app review.
- **Why not just ChatGPT?** It's a whole pipeline: find, verify, score, make the sample, write, judge, post, all on its own at night with safety checks, in Kurdish.
- **Privacy?** Public business information only, never private people. Each customer has their own private Jump HQ.
- **How do you make money if the AI costs money?** The customer pays monthly and we pay the AI. Plan limits cap each customer's cost, and the owner view shows cost against revenue per customer.
