# Jump HQ: master brief (internal)

Jump HQ is a public online service: an AI team that finds clients and runs social media for
Sulaimani's online businesses and freelancers. Customers sign up on the website, get their own
private Jump HQ and pay monthly: free week, **Pro $20/month**, **Agency $60/month**.

- Product, features and how it's built: [README.md](README.md)
- Pitch, numbers and judge Q&A: [PITCH.md](PITCH.md)
- Hosting: `Host Jump HQ.bat` (public Cloudflare link + sign-up cap) or Docker / `render.yaml`
- Owner: Ramyar (Jump, jumpagency.org)

History: started 2026-10-06 as a single-owner web design agency tool (Scout → Builder → Writer → Closer).
Grow mode, the Judge, Sleep mode, the phone app, three languages and Jump HQ Cloud were built at the
SmartSuli hackathon, Oct 8–9.
